A monthly painting competition for the group — submit what you've painted, vote on everyone else's, and climb the yearly leaderboard.

How it works

  • Each month, submit a model you've painted via Submit Entry — your name, the month, your model's name, and at least one photo.
  • Once every entry is in for the month, the organiser opens voting. Head to Vote, register your name, and score every entry from 1 to 5.
  • You can only see and browse entries while voting is locked — scoring unlocks once the organiser opens the month.
  • You can only submit your scores once per month. If you need a correction, ask the organiser.
  • Check Results for how a given month shook out, and the Leaderboard for the running yearly standings.
